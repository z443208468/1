The River Thief

A five-stop Tokyo mystery · One river. Two names. One escape you will see through.

The river fills the little stage. A dark screen sweeps across the window. When the window clears, the river is gone. Only a caption remains: CHITOSE-ZA SCENE / INASE RIVER.

At five real places, reconstruct the views and find his exit. Make him try the same trick again—with both the river and its thief in view.
